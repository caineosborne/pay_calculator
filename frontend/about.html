About this calculator

This page explains what the calculator does, how to use it and the limits of its results.


PayGuide is an independent project and is not affiliated with the Fair Work Ombudsman or the Fair Work Commission. Outputs are provided as guidance only and should not be treated as legal advice.

1. How the calculator works

The calculator is a simplified version of the selected award. It is designed to estimate pay for hours worked across a fortnight. Any entitlements that apply for a time period longer than a fortnight are not included in the calculations.

The calculator only applies the configured pay rules shown for the selected award. It does not calculate allowances, reimbursements, leave, leave loading, superannuation, tax, deductions, termination payments, interest or back pay, or any other entitlements.

Using the calculator

  1. Select the applicable award or enterprise agreement from the navigation bar.
  2. Select the employee’s classification and hourly rate, or enter the correct hourly rate manually.
  3. Choose the employment type: full-time, part-time or casual.
  4. For a part-time employee, enter their agreed contracted hours per week.
  5. Choose whether the employee is a day worker or shiftworker.
  6. Enter the start and finish time for each shift in the fortnight.
  7. Edit the lunch break start time and duration where relevant.
  8. If required, manually mark a day as overtime (OT), ordinary time (ORD), or a public holiday.
  9. Review the calculated hours and pay against your own records and payslip.
